## Event 2: Labor Day Weekend Traffic + FrontRunner Service Disruption

### Details
| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| **Date** | Friday Aug 31 -- Monday Sep 3, 2018 (Labor Day Weekend) |
| **Peak Outbound** | Friday Aug 31, 2:00 PM -- 8:30 PM (peak ~5:00 PM) |
| **Peak Return** | Monday Sep 3, 10:00 AM -- 9:00 PM |
| **Duration** | 4-day weekend |

### Specific Traffic Impacts

**I-15 through Salt Lake and Utah Counties:**
- Up to an **extra hour** of travel time on Friday between 2:00 and 8:30 PM
- Heaviest delays expected around 5:00 PM Friday
- Heavy northbound I-15 congestion Monday from 10:00 AM onward

**I-15 Technology Corridor (Lehi):**
- Northbound lanes split from 2100 North to State Road 92
- Reduced speed limit
- Rough/uneven pavement

**UDOT Construction Suspension:**
- UDOT suspended most projects Friday through Monday, opening all lanes
- Some lane restrictions remained for work zone protection

### FrontRunner Commuter Rail Disruption (Saturday Sep 1)
- **Northern segment between Ogden and North Temple station** in Salt Lake City **suspended** for the entire day
- Reason: Federal control system testing (Positive Train Control) to prevent trains from overshooting stop signals
- UTA offered bus/van replacement service from FrontRunner stations
- Sunday returned to normal; Monday operated on Saturday schedule

## Event 13: I-15 Multi-Vehicle Crash (Draper)

### Exact Details
| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| **Date** | Thursday, September 20, 2018 |
| **Time** | Morning rush hour (~7:30 AM -- 11:00 AM MDT) |
| **Duration** | Approximately 3.5 hours |
| **Location** | I-15 Southbound at 14400 South, Draper, Utah |
| **Reporting Officer** | Utah Highway Patrol Sgt. Chad Hecker |

### Description
A four-vehicle collision during morning rush hour. A Volkswagen Passat rear-ended an SUV, which was pushed into two other vehicles. The SUV ended up **on top of** the Passat. Multiple southbound lanes of I-15 were blocked during emergency response and cleanup.

### Traffic Impact
- Multiple **southbound I-15 lanes blocked** at 14400 South
- Morning rush hour disruption (~7:30 AM onward)
- Scene cleared and traffic restored by approximately **11:00 AM**
- Significant backup along I-15 southbound through Draper/South Jordan

### Injuries
All minor. One female passenger transported to hospital as a precaution. All occupants were wearing seatbelts.
